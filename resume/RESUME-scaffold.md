## On "answers AI acceptance"

Employers adding this screen are usually asking one of two questions, and they're
different:

- **"Will this person use the tools?"** — the fear is a hire who refuses them or
  quietly avoids them. Answered by: the named section above, and one concrete tool +
  task + result.
- **"Will this person use them badly?"** — the fear is unchecked output going to a
  client, a customer, or a regulator. Answered by: the *Where I don't* line. Most
  candidates skip it. It's the strongest line on the page for exactly that reason.

If the job description (once you send it) leans heavily on the first, expand the
section to 5 lines. If it's a regulated or client-facing role, the second line matters
more than the first.

## On "human worded"

The scaffold deliberately avoids the patterns that read as machine-written:

- No three-item parallel lists in every sentence
- No sentence that opens with a participial phrase ("Leveraging my background in…")
- Varied bullet length — some short, some two lines
- Plain verbs: *built, ran, fixed, cut, wrote, handled, took over*
- Contractions in the AI section, because that section is you talking

One practical check before you send it: read the Summary and the AI section out loud.
If you wouldn't say it that way to a person across a table, rewrite it.
